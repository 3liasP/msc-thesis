The topic of this thesis started as part of a proof-of-concept study, driven by curiosity to learn about artificial intelligence and explore how it could be applied to solve real problems in Sovelia Core, a PLM software I have been contributing to as a software developer for a couple of years now. From just a proof of concept, the idea grew and was even demonstrated to customers, garnering significant interest despite its quite simple initial state. This thesis is about productizing that proof of concept and addressing all the enterprise requirements that come with it.

I have genuinely enjoyed my time developing this feature, and I am both keen and a bit terrified to see how it performs when it is actually deployed in live customer environments.
